### ★ Panel I — strongest, most defensible finding

Scheduling-fraction-of-wall-time (`sched / cluster_parallel`):

- Crosses **~50% by N≈4–8** for D=20k / 40k / 50k
- Converges to **>90% by N=32**

This is a **direct ratio** — no fitted vertex, no argmin. Orchestration dominates almost immediately, not only “at high N.” **Lead with Panel I in the paper.**

### Panel E — argmin fallacy (do not publish flat plateau)

| D | Discrete argmin (pow2) | Per-D quadratic N* | Global T(N,D) N* | Paper N* |
|---|------------------------|--------------------|------------------|----------|
| 10k | 5 | non-convex (γ≤0) | clamped low | 114 |
| 20k | **16** | **~35** | grows with D | 128 |
| 40k | **16** | **~11** | ~9 | 156 |
| 50k | **16** | non-convex (N=32 spike) | **~26** | 170 |

Connecting discrete argmins (2,4,8,16,32,…) produces a **false flat “N*=16 plateau”**. That is a **sampling-grid artifact**, not a physical vertex.

**Publish instead:** fitted vertices from global `T(N,D)` (and/or per-D quadratic when γ>0). Direction matches the paper (N* increases with D); **magnitude is far smaller** than paper’s 107–170.

## Part 9 — N* math doc

```markdown
# N* (optimal nodes) — paper model vs our experiments

**Paper:** *Optimal Resource Allocation for Distributed Descriptor Computation in Cheminformatics*  
**PDF transcription:** `data/paper_execution_times.json` (Tables 1–2, page 4)  
**Code:** `src/core/model.py` → `ModelCoefficients.optimal_nodes()`

---

## 1. The paper’s model

End-to-end execution time:

\[
T(N,D) = a + bN + cD + dN^2 + eND
\]

| Coefficient | Paper fit (PDF tables, R²=0.851) |
|-------------|----------------------------------|
| a | 264.35 |
| b | −4.0446 |
| c | 0.01234 |
| d | **0.02031** |
| e | −5.716×10⁻⁵ |

---

## 2. Derivative and interior optimum N*(D)

Treat **D fixed**, differentiate w.r.t. **N**:

\[
\frac{\partial T}{\partial N} = b + 2dN + eD
\]

**First-order condition (FOC):** set ∂T/∂N = 0

\[
\boxed{N^*(D) = -\frac{b + eD}{2d}}
\]

**Second derivative:**

\[
\frac{\partial^2 T}{\partial N^2} = 2d
\]

Interior minimum exists iff **d > 0** (convex parabola in N).

**Paper @ D=50,000:**

\[
N^*(50000) = -\frac{-4.0446 + (-5.716\times10^{-5})(50000)}{2(0.02031)} \approx 169.9 \;\Rightarrow\; 170
\]

---

## 3. Paper N*(D) table (analytical)

| D | N*(paper) | T(N*) predicted |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 5,000 | **107** | 95 s |
| 10,000 | **114** | 126 s |
| 20,000 | **128** | 180 s |
| 30,000 | **142** | 226 s |
| 40,000 | **156** | 265 s |
| 50,000 | **170** | 295 s |

**Trend:** N* **increases** with D (because e < 0: larger datasets tolerate more nodes before orchestration penalty dominates).

Paper experiments only tested **N ∈ {25, 35, …, 185}** — so N* ≈ 107–170 lies inside the grid.

---

## 4. Paper tables: analytical N* vs empirical argmin

Empirical argmin = N with minimum transcribed execution time at fixed D.

| D | N_emp (PDF tables) | T_emp | N*(paper) | Match? |
|---|-------------------|-------|-----------|--------|
| 5k | 75 | 131 s | 107 | ~ |
| 10k | 145 | 152 s | 114 | ✗ |
| 20k | 145 | 183 s | 128 | ~ |
| 30k | 175 | 243 s | 142 | ~ |
| 40k | 145 | 251 s | 156 | ~ |
| 50k | **185** (boundary!) | 308 s | 170 | ✗ (min at edge) |

**Per-D quadratic fit** on paper data alone (T(N) = α + βN + γN² → N* = −β/(2γ)):

| D | N*_1D | N*_global | N_emp |
|---|-------|-----------|-------|
| 5k | 113 | 107 | 75 |
| 10k | 128 | 114 | 145 |
| 50k | **145** | **170** | **185** |

The global 2D model **overshoots** N* at D=50k (170 vs per-D 145). At D=50k the empirical minimum is at **N=185** (grid edge) — the true optimum may be **≥185**, so the parabolic model’s N*=170 is an interior prediction that doesn’t match the observed edge minimum.

---

## 5. What **we** measured (2026 probes)

### Metric difference (critical)

| Source | Metric | N range |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Paper PDF | Full end-to-end (cluster + scheduling + compute + sync) | 25–185 |
| Overnight grid | `wall_clock` (S3 + init + cluster_parallel) | 25–125 |
| Pow2 probes | `cluster_parallel` only | **2–128** |

### Empirical optima (our data)

| Experiment | D | N_emp | T_emp | Paper N* |
|------------|---|-------|-------|----------|
| probe D=10k Spot low | 10k | **5** | 17 s | 114 |
| probe D=50k Spot low | 50k | **16** | 106 s | 170 |
| probe D=50k Spot medium | 50k | **8** | 179 s | 170 |
| overnight Spot low | 50k | **25** | 152 s (wall) | 170 |

### Can we apply N*(D) to our data?

**Global OLS on our overnight wall_clock (300 pts):** fitted **d < 0** → **no interior minimum** in the paper model form. Within N≥25, time is mostly **monotone increasing** in N.

**Global OLS on probe D=50k low (14 pts, single D):** **d < 0** again — the U-shape minimum at N=16 is **not captured** by a single convex parabola over N=2…128 (N=32 spike breaks symmetry).

**Per-D quadratic on probe medium D=50k:** γ > 0 → N*_1D ≈ **58**, but empirical min is **N=8**. Model misfit because scheduling spike at N=32+ dominates the fit.

---

## 6. What we have vs what it should be

### What the paper has (correct math)

- Model T(N,D) and **N*(D) = −(b+eD)/(2d)** are **mathematically correct** given d > 0.
- Coefficients fitted to PDF Tables 1–2 (R²=0.85).
- Predicts **high N* (107–170)** for D=5k–50k when N is explored only above 25.

### What our experiments show

- The U-curve minimum sits at **much lower N** when you sweep N=2–16:
  - D=10k → N*=5 (not 114)
  - D=50k → N*=8–16 (not 170)
- **Orchestration** (scheduling stagger) creates the U-shape; **compute** scales perfectly as 1/N.
- Paper N* formula **does not transfer** to our cluster_parallel metric without refitting.

### What it **should** be (recommendations)

1. **Refit T(N,D) on `wall_clock`** including **low N** (2, 4, 8, 16, …) — current paper fit is blind to N < 25.

2. **Use per-D fit** when reporting N* for a single dataset size:
   \[
   T(N) = \alpha + \beta N + \gamma N^2,\quad N^* = -\frac{\beta}{2\gamma}\quad (\gamma > 0)
   \]

3. **Update paper narrative:** N*(D) increases with D **only within the tested orchestration regime** (N ≥ 25). With modern Batch + warm CE, optimum shifts **left** to N ≈ 5–16 for D ≤ 50k.

4. **Practical N* (empirical, our harness):**

   | D | Spot low | Spot medium |
   |---|----------|-------------|
   | 10k | **5** | (not run) |
   | 50k | **16** | **8** |

5. **Paper’s N*=170 @ D=50k** is a **legacy high-N recommendation** — still valid if you only allow N ≥ 25, but **suboptimal by ~40% wall time** vs N=16 in our probes.

## 7. Quick reference — derivative steps (Section 5 equivalent)

```
T(N,D) = a + bN + cD + dN² + eND

∂T/∂N = b + 2dN + eD     ← linear in N
∂²T/∂N² = 2d               ← constant; need d > 0 for minimum

Set ∂T/∂N = 0:
    2dN = -(b + eD)
    N*(D) = -(b + eD) / (2d)

Verify minimum: ∂²T/∂N² = 2d > 0 ✓ (paper: 2d ≈ 0.0406)
```
